APPROVED VISUAL SYSTEM — MUST FOLLOW:
ONLY THREE base colors throughout: soft forest green #527A63, warm ivory #F5F3EC, deep charcoal green #25382E. Derive all tones ONLY using opacity and blends of these three. No orange, blue, yellow, purple, red, gold or pure black/white accents, including in stars, photos, badges and charts. Greys are blends of approved colors. Muted soft color, not saturated emerald or neon. Dark charcoal text for readable contrast. Main actions use green with ivory lettering.
Airy sophisticated card-based bento composition, generous whitespace and breathing room. Small corner radii: roughly 8px cards, 6px controls; no giant pill-shaped cards. Restrained frosted glass on navigation or overlapping surfaces with subtle Gaussian background blur, translucent ivory surfaces, gentle green-to-transparent gradients, extremely fine barely-visible noise texture. Subtle low-contrast ambient shadows produce depth without heavy decoration. Mostly stable opaque surfaces behind text. No noisy patterned background, no huge decorative blobs, no ornamental 3D graphics.
Clear strong information hierarchy: very readable modern Simplified Chinese sans-serif like PingFang SC / Noto Sans SC. Consistent type scale: 24 logical px page heading, 18px section title, 16px body/button, 13px metadata minimum. Regular 400, medium 500 and semibold 600 weights; no serif or calligraphy. Consistent alignment and spacing rhythm 8/16/24. Touch actions about 44 logical px tall.
Icons must look like clean SVG line icons: consistent 24px square, 1.75px stroke, rounded linecaps, monochrome green/charcoal, no emoji or stock colored pictograms. SVG source assets are delivered separately; the picture is a raster mockup. Show short useful visualizations ONLY for real UI information: a simple quote comparison, a 3-step task status indicator, and three independent five-star rating rows. No invented activity charts, popularity counts or business achievements.
Every screen must visibly include "设计示例". All names are explicitly generic demo labels, all areas displayed as "示例区域" or "选择村/社区"; do not invent actual township names, street addresses, real merchants, real photos of people, GPS distances, or live operational data. Sample photos of an appliance, a bicycle and a parcel, if used, must be rendered as muted monochrome green/ivory studio thumbnails within the three-color palette.
